# Platform risk composition

## Language

**Contract premium**: The cost quoted and signed by the insurer, booked under the insured party's perspective. It changes through a signed re-quote and adoption.
_Avoid_: assessed uplift, bill inferred from a control observation

**Technical condition assessment**: A dated, source-bound assessment of a quoted control against the declared served subjects and policies. Its states are met, breached and unobserved; it establishes neither legal payability nor transferred loss.
_Avoid_: implementation count, insurance approval

**Modeled conditional cost**: The contract premium plus any once-only signed increments supported by admitted technical breaches, evaluated under the insured perspective. An unresolved condition makes the effective conditional cost unknown while the known contract premium remains visible.
_Avoid_: current bill, actuarial loss

**Observation selector**: The insured party's reference to one immutable scheduled observation and an explicit examined instant. It selects evidence for independent authentication and supplies no authority by itself.
_Avoid_: admitted observation, evidence approval
